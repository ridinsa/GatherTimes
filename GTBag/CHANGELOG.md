# GTBag

## 0.25.3 (2026-11-23)

- New: one-window bag browser (`B` or `/gtbag`), with icon grid, search, category and bag-slot views, and five window styles.
- New: bank, guild bank, mail and every character's bags in one window, with saved data when you are away.
- New: right-click a section header (Stack, Columns or List style) to move that section to the bank or bags, or mail it to another character.
- New: sorting and merging, drag-order in category view, pinned and hidden items, and custom categories with a category editor.
- New: item tooltips with known counts across your characters, banks, mail and auction scans.
- Fix: no false "your bags are full" message after a teleport or loading screen.
- New: sell junk button and optional auto-sell (off by default), with a keep list.
- New: optional auto sort when a vendor opens, when a bank opens, or after combat (Settings > Sort; all off by default).
- New: the character selector is compact: each character shows its name and gold, silver and copper.
- New: privacy toggle key (Key Bindings > AddOns > GTBag) and a "Privacy on at login" option. Privacy starts off after each login or reload unless that option is ticked. Privacy also scrambles the names on Blizzard's player, character and pet frames, and hides your own and your pet's name above their heads. The same switch also hides session gold in GatherTimes and turns on its name disguise.
- New: privacy mode that scrambles names and gold, backup and restore as plain text, and a small public API (`GTBagAPI`).
- New: works with GatherTimes for shared fade setting, skin theme and auction scan prices, and runs on its own without it.
